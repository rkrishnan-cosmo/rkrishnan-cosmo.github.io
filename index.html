Research in brief

Galaxy clusters constrain dark energy (w), the matter density (Ωm), and the clustering amplitude (σ8). SZ- and X-ray-selected samples agree well with the CMB, but optically selected clusters are affected by projection effects along the line of sight.

My work addresses that problem: measuring orientation bias with BCG shapes, fitting triaxial cluster shapes with weak lensing, and building a shear-ratio estimator that probes the expansion history while depending only weakly on the lens mass.
